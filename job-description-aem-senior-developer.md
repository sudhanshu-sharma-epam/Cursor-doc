# Senior AEM Developer — Sample job description

**Company:** [Organization name]  
**Location:** [Hybrid / Remote / On-site — city]  
**Employment type:** Full-time  
**Department:** Digital / Marketing Technology / Engineering

## Role summary

We are looking for a **Senior Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Developer** to design, build, and maintain enterprise content and experience solutions on **AEM as a Cloud Service (AEMaaCS)** or **AEM 6.5+**. You will partner with architects, backend engineers, QA, and marketing stakeholders to deliver scalable components, integrations, and release-ready code.

## What you will do

- Lead implementation of **AEM Sites** (and **Assets** where applicable): editable templates, **Sling Models**, **HTL**, OSGi services, workflows, and launchers.
- Own **frontend integration** for authorable experiences: component libraries, responsive layouts, accessibility, and performance budgets.
- Define and enforce **development standards**: code review, branching strategy, CI/CD hooks, and quality gates (SonarQube, linting, automated tests).
- Collaborate on **integrations** with analytics, personalization, search, DAM, identity (SSO/SAML/OIDC), and headless or hybrid delivery patterns (**Content Fragments**, **GraphQL**) when required.
- Troubleshoot **production** issues: dispatcher/cache behavior, replication, indexing, and cloud pipeline failures; document runbooks and mitigations.
- Mentor **mid-level and junior** developers; contribute to estimates, technical spikes, and architecture discussions.

## Required qualifications

- **5+ years** of professional software development experience, including **3+ years** hands-on with **AEM** in large or regulated environments.
- Strong **Java** and AEM stack: **Sling**, **JCR**, **OSGi**, **HTL**, **Sling Models**, **Apache Felix**.
- Solid **frontend** skills: **HTML/CSS/JavaScript**, modern tooling (e.g. Webpack/Vite), and integration with design systems or component frameworks.
- Experience with **AEMaaCS** or **AEM 6.5** including **Cloud Manager** pipelines, **Dispatcher**, and **Maven** multi-module projects.
- Understanding of **SEO**, **WCAG** accessibility, and **Core Web Vitals** as they relate to authored pages.
- Excellent **communication** and stakeholder management; comfortable leading technical discussions in English [adjust if needed].

## Preferred qualifications

- **Adobe certifications** (e.g. AEM Sites Developer, Business Practitioner) or equivalent demonstrable depth.
- Experience with **AEM Forms**, **Adobe Target / Analytics** integration, or **Adobe I/O** extensibility.
- **DevOps** familiarity: Docker, Kubernetes concepts, infrastructure-as-code, or observability (logs, metrics, tracing).
- Background in **financial services, healthcare, or retail** with compliance-aware delivery practices.

## What we offer

- Competitive compensation and benefits package [customize].
- Learning budget and conference attendance for Adobe and web platform topics.
- Collaborative engineering culture with clear ownership and technical leadership paths.
